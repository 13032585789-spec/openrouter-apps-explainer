    Popular rewards size. Trending rewards growth.

    Popular ranks by total tokens in the window. Trending ranks by the window's volume minus the average of the three earlier periods of equal length, and leaves out apps with no excess growth [3]. The three apps and all numbers are invented, in billions of tokens. Open the editor to change them.

    What Infron could take from it

    My read, not a forecast. None of this is tested.

    Make public listing opt-in

    OpenRouter lists a new app publicly unless it sends "hidden". Infron's buyers care about data governance, so the default should be private, with the choice explained from the Trust Center.

    Metric: opt-in rate among builders asked

    Per-app analytics before a leaderboard

    With few apps a ranking looks thin. Give each API key an app page with usage by model and route first, and add a Trending view once there are enough apps for it to mean something.

    Metric: share of active keys that send an app header

    One small dataset, with a citation line

    For example provider retention and training policy by route, taken from the Trust Center, with no customer data. Show an updated date, since stale policy data is the risk.

    Metric: referring domains and docs visits
